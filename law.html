// The Problem

WHAT'S SITTING
IN YOUR STORAGE
RIGHT NOW

Law firms rotate equipment on 3–5 year cycles. The old devices don't disappear — they accumulate. And every one of them is a liability.

💼
Retired Attorney Laptops

Case files, client correspondence, privileged work product — all sitting on drives that got replaced but never wiped. Confidentiality obligations don't end at device retirement.

🖥️
Old Workstations & Servers

On-premise servers from prior years hold client databases, billing records, and matter files. When you upgrade, the old hardware rarely gets properly destroyed.

📁
No Destruction Documentation

Bar associations increasingly expect documented data handling procedures. "We recycled it" is not documentation. A Certificate of Destruction is.

🔓
Vendor Risk

Most recycling companies are logistics operations — not security firms. You don't know how your client data was handled after pickup. We are cybersecurity professionals first.
